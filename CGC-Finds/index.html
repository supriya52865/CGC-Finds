The more detail you add, the better the automatic matching works.

How matches are found

  1. Your item name and description are reduced to keywords. Filler words are dropped and similar words are treated as one, so "mobile" and "phone" count as the same.
  2. A lost report is compared with found reports, and the other way round.
  3. The score combines shared keywords, the same category and a shared place. Anything of 30% or more is suggested.
